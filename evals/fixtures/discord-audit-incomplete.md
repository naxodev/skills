# Fictional Discord audit snapshot

The owner requests an audit only. The community has 20 invited beta users and two maintainers.
Product surfaces are command execution and build previews. Support and bugs belong in the issue tracker.
Security reports belong in the project's private security-reporting form. Conduct reports go to the human owner.

Recorded configuration:

- Community is enabled.
- General, announcements, support, and staff channels exist.
- Announcements deny baseline members messages, replies in existing threads, public and private thread creation, polls, and invite creation.
- The support forum requires at least one member-selectable tag: command execution or build previews. Workflow-state tags are moderator-only.
- The interest role grants only the relevant product channels and notifications, with no privileged permissions.
- Supplied role preview hides staff and unselected opt-in channels from the baseline member. It confirms the recorded interest-role permission shape.
- No fresh human test account is available. Posting and tag enforcement were not tested as a real member.
- Onboarding, Rules Screening, voice, AutoMod behavior, and recovery arrangements were not observed.
- No integrations were approved. No invite or credential is included in this snapshot.

Only these observations are available. Browser and account access are unavailable.
Exact current Discord feature labels require live official documentation and remain unverified in this offline fixture.
